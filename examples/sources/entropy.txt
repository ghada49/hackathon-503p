Project-authored teaching note. Nonnegative outcome weights w_i with a strictly positive total are normalized to probabilities p_i = w_i / sum_j(w_j). Discrete Shannon entropy in bits is H = -sum_i(p_i*log2(p_i)), with the contribution at p_i=0 defined as zero by continuity. A uniform distribution on n outcomes has entropy log2(n). A distribution concentrated on one outcome has entropy zero. Use independently editable outcome weights and a logarithm base or concentration/mixture control to compare distributions; convert entropy units consistently when changing the base. Avoid evaluating log(0) before handling the zero contribution; a small protected logarithm argument can be a clearly disclosed numerical simplification. The probabilities sum to one and entropy is nonnegative. This is distributional uncertainty, not a measure of semantic meaning or evidence strength.
